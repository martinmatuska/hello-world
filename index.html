Frequently asked

How does Matcha Bliss taste?

Smooth, creamy, and naturally sweet with a clean matcha finish. The coconut and oat base gives it a latte-like texture without any dairy. Most customers say it tastes better than café matcha lattes — and it's under 1€ per cup.

How much caffeine does it contain?

Approximately 25mg per serving — about 3x less than a regular cup of coffee (~95mg). Combined with L-theanine naturally present in matcha, the energy release is gentle and sustained without jitters or an afternoon crash.

Is it suitable for pregnant or breastfeeding women?

We recommend consulting your healthcare provider before consuming Matcha Bliss during pregnancy or breastfeeding, as it contains caffeine and adaptogenic herbs. It is not recommended for children.

Is it vegan and gluten-free?

Yes! Matcha Bliss is 100% plant-based and dairy-free. The oat base may contain trace gluten — check the label if you have coeliac disease. All ingredients are organic, with no artificial colours, flavours, or preservatives.

How many servings are in one pack?

Each pack contains 30 full servings (8g per serving). That's a full month of daily rituals for just 0,93€ per cup — a fraction of what you'd pay at a coffee shop.

Can I prepare it cold?

Absolutely! Add one scoop to cold water or plant milk, shake well or blend with ice, and enjoy a refreshing iced matcha latte. Perfect for warm days or as a post-workout drink.
